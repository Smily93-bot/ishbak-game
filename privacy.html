Ishbak Privacy Policy

Game data

Ishbak does not require an account. Player names and scores are used during the local game session and are not sent to an Ishbak database. The game does not use device motion for controls.

Advertising

The Android app may use Google AdMob and the website may use Google AdSense. Google may process device identifiers, cookies, or similar data according to consent choices and advertising settings. See Google's Privacy Policy.
